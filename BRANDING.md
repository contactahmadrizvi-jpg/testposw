## Visual Identity

### Logo Usage
- The logo should always maintain clear space around it
- Minimum size: 40px height for digital
- Always use on white or light backgrounds for best contrast

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: DM Sans
- **Headings**: Black (900 weight)
- **Body**: Regular to Semibold (400-600)

### Gradient Text Effect
```css
background: linear-gradient(to right, #3b82f6, #2563eb);
-webkit-background-clip: text;
-webkit-text-fill-color: transparent;
```

### Border Radius
- Standard: 12px (0.75rem)
- Large cards: 16px (1rem)
- Pills/badges: Full rounded

## Brand Evolution

### Previous Identity
- **Name**: Rush Pizza and Burger
- **Theme**: Red/Orange with warm beige tones
- **Style**: Casual, rustic

### Current Identity
- **Name**: SOMO
- **Theme**: Blue gradients with pure white
- **Style**: Modern, professional, clean

### Reasoning
The rebrand to SOMO represents:
- **Simplicity**: Easy to remember, easy to say
- **Modernity**: Clean, contemporary design
- **Professionalism**: Trustworthy and reliable
- **Versatility**: Works for various cuisines
- **Scalability**: Ready for growth and expansion

## Usage Guidelines

### DO's ✅
- Use consistent blue gradients
- Maintain clean white backgrounds
- Apply modern rounded corners
- Use bold, clear typography
- Keep messaging simple and direct

### DON'Ts ❌
- Don't alter the blue color scheme
- Don't use cluttered designs
- Don't mix conflicting styles
- Don't use outdated food imagery
- Don't compromise on white space
